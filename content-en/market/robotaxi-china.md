## Apollo Go (Baidu Apollo Go)

**Order volume (mainly by financial-report account)**
- In 2025Q2, fully driverless orders exceeded 2.2 million, up +148% year on year, and cumulative orders exceeded 14 million as of August 2025 (Baidu 2025Q2 financial report). Source: [Jiemian News relaying the financial report (2025-08-20)](https://www.jiemian.com/article/13209061.html)
- As of 2025-10-31: weekly orders exceeded 250,000, cumulative orders exceeded 17 million, covering 22 cities (company disclosure).
- 2026Q2 (financial report released 2026-08-18): fully driverless orders in the quarter were about 1 million; management said this was temporarily affected by regulatory-related operational adjustments in some domestic cities, with affected areas resuming from August; as of June 2026, cumulative orders exceeded 23 million, covering 28 cities; cumulative autonomous driving mileage exceeded 350 million km. Source: [Baidu official results announcement (HKEX, 2026-08-18)](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0818/2026081801093_c.pdf)
- 2026Q3 (financial report released 2026-11-16): the financial report did not disclose order volume for the quarter and instead reported mileage — autonomous driving mileage in the quarter was about 34 million km, cumulative exceeding 390 million km (as of 2026-10-31), with a global footprint of 30 cities; fully driverless orders in Shenzhen rose by about +170% quarter on quarter (no absolute figure).
- Note: "2025Q4 single-quarter fully driverless 3.4 million rides" comes from the original text of Baidu's Q4 2025 financial report (2026-02-26): "delivered 3.4 million fully driverless operational rides". Source: [Baidu Q4 2025 financial report (SEC filing)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1329099/000119312526076505/d117608dex991.htm)

**Wuhan 2024: scaling and controversy**
- On 2024-05-15 Baidu announced the sixth-generation vehicle would be deployed in Wuhan, completing deployment of 1,000 vehicles within the year【Company claim: target】; in July 2024 the Wuhan Municipal Transport Bureau clarified that the actual deployment was more than 400 vehicles, not the rumoured 1,000 online; during the same period there were congestion complaints and discussion of ride-hailing drivers' livelihoods. Source: [Cailian Press](https://www.cls.cn/detail/1691088)

**Overseas expansion (item by item, varying degrees of verification)**
- **Hong Kong**【Verified】: In November 2024 it obtained Hong Kong's first autonomous vehicle pilot licence (10 vehicles, North Lantau), and began road testing in December 2024; it has since expanded multiple times to Airport Island, Tung Chung, Cyberport in the Southern District of Hong Kong Island, and Kowloon East; in February 2026 cross-district operation Airport Island—Tung Chung—Sunny Bay was approved. Source: [Hong Kong Transport Department official page](https://www.td.gov.hk/sc/public_services/taoav/index.html)
- **Dubai**【Verified】: In March 2025 it signed a cooperation agreement with the RTA, in July it obtained Dubai's first autonomous driving test licence, and 50 RT6 vehicles began public-road testing from August 2025; in January 2026 it obtained Dubai's first fully driverless (no safety driver) test permit (verified against RTA and Baidu's official release); "expanding to 1,000+ vehicles by 2028" is a plan account. Source: [Baidu official release (PR Newswire, 2026-01-07)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/baidus-apollo-go-secures-dubais-first-fully-driverless-testing-permit-launches-local-operations-hub-302654927.html)
- **Switzerland**【Verified: partner official】: In partnership with PostBus under the brand AmiGo, testing with a safety driver began in December 2025; in 2026-06 it obtained a FEDRO special permit and began hands-free testing in about 80 km² in eastern Switzerland (with a safety driver still in the vehicle). Source: [PostBus official (2026-06-12)](https://www.postauto.ch/en/about-us-and-news/news/2026/milestone-for-autonomous-mobility-in-switzerland)
- **Platform partnerships** (company announcements): In July 2025 it announced a multi-year partnership with Uber (outside the US and mainland China, Asia and the Middle East first); in August it announced a partnership with Lyft (Germany and the UK first).

## Pony.ai

- Listing: Listed on Nasdaq on 2024-11-27 (PONY), raising about 260 million USD (299 million with full exercise of the over-allotment option); on 2025-11-06 it completed a dual primary listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (2026.HK), raising about 6.707 billion HKD. Source: [Nasdaq official release (2024-11-27)](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/ponyai-lists-nasdaq-2024-11-27)
- Operating permits: After Shanghai issued a new batch of demonstration operation licences in 2025-07, Pony.ai launched fully driverless commercial operations in Pudong; the company says it thereby holds commercialisation permits in all four cities of Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen (the grading definitions differ by city; Shanghai is demonstration operation, see [[companies/pony-ai|Pony.ai]]). Source: [Securities Times](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/2904979.html)
- Seventh-generation Robotaxi【Company claim】: Released in April 2025, kit BOM cost down 70% compared with the previous generation; partner models are GAC Aion, BAIC Arcfox, and Toyota bZ; in 2025-11 it launched fully driverless commercial operations in Beijing, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen, with more than 600 mass-production vehicles rolled off the line. Source: [Cailian Press](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2232067)
- Scale (by company target account, not verified inventory): In November 2025 the fleet exceeded 900 vehicles; the 2026 target has been revised up from 3,000+ to 3,500+ vehicles by year-end (2026-05, see the Pony.ai article).

The company's self-description as "the world's first listed general autonomous driving company" is **promotional language**. ## Other players: two different routes

- **DiDi Autonomous Driving**: A 50/50 joint venture with GAC Aion (business licence obtained in April 2024); its first front-installed mass-production L4 model was released in 2025-04 (10 lidars), planned to join DiDi's ride-hailing platform via hybrid dispatch in parts of Guangzhou/Beijing in 2026【Company claim: target】.
- **Momenta**: Does not operate; it is a mass-production assisted driving supplier — as of the end of 2025, cumulative design wins were 170 models, of which 68 had entered mass production (prospectus account); the common claim of "more than 160 models in mass production" mistakes the design-win count for the mass-production count; for its technology route, see [[technology/stack|Technology stack overview]].
- **Huawei Qiankun Smart Driving**: ADS 4 was released on 2025-04-22 (including a highway L3 commercial solution), with large-scale rollout from September 2025; ADS 5 was released on 2026-04-23. Note that "L3 commercial solution" is product language and is a different matter from regulatory access (see the China L3 section in [[regulation/levels-and-rules|Levels and regulation]]). Source: [Huawei official website](https://auto.huawei.com/cn/news/2025/huawei-qiankun-intelligent-technology-launch/)
- **Automaker city navigation**: Self-compiled statistics by Auto Commune say city NOA (Navigate on Autopilot) insurance registrations in 2025 totalled 2.67 million vehicles for the full year, with a penetration rate of 11.6% (excluding Tesla FSD; different in definition from the CAAM report's 3.129 million vehicles / 15.1% for January–November 2025 and not comparable side by side, see [[market/consumer-adas|Consumer ADAS]]). The routes of individual companies have diverged (XPeng vision-only + VLA, NIO world model, etc.); the details come from industry roundups and have not been verified item by item against automaker official releases, so this page records only the fact of divergence; for route details, see [[technology/routes|The route debate]].

## Policy framework: national pilots + local legislation

- **2023-11-17** Four departments issued the *Notice on Carrying Out Pilot Work for Access and Road Use of Intelligent Connected Vehicles* (MIIT et al. [2023] No. 217) — the overall framework for L3/L4 access pilots. Source: [MIIT original text](https://www.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/zbys/wjfb/art/2023/art_4a67648dc58e483bab554f97045a8579.html)
- **2024-06-04** The first batch of 9 pilot consortia was announced (Changan, BYD, GAC, SAIC, BAIC BluePark Magna, FAW, SAIC Hongyan, Yutong, NIO); officials stressed at the time that this only completed application and selection and **did not mean access had been obtained**.
- **2024-07-03** The list of 20 pilot cities (consortia) for "vehicle-road-cloud integration" was announced, including Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chongqing, Wuhan, etc. Source: [MIIT original text](https://www.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/wjfb/tz/art/2024/art_7b42963f0fde4af496dfcc9c224be54c.html)
- **Local regulations**: Beijing's *Autonomous Vehicle Regulations* took effect on 2025-04-01 (covering L3 and above); Shanghai Pudong's driverless regulations took effect on 2023-02-01; the *Wuhan Regulations on Promoting the Development of Intelligent Connected Vehicles* took effect on 2025-03-01, including provisions on accident handling for driverless vehicles.
- **Formal L3 landing**: On 2025-12-15 the first two models obtained conditional access, on 2025-12-23 Beijing issued the first batch of L3 dedicated licence plates, and in 2026-10 officials confirmed the second batch of pilots was "under way" — this line is listed separately in [[regulation/levels-and-rules|Autonomous driving levels and regulation]].

## Comparison with the US market

- **Speed difference**: The order volumes of China's leading robotaxis are already in the same order of magnitude (Apollo Go cumulative 23 million rides vs Waymo cumulative 20 million trips; the definitions are similar but not fully comparable), and overseas destinations overlap heavily (the Middle East, Europe, Singapore).
- **Path difference**: The US is a "patchwork of state-level permits + federal vehicle standards"; China is a "national pilot framework + city operating permits + local legislation", with the permit pace driven by administrative pilots rather than spontaneously by the market.
- **Common ground**: Both sides have been stuck on the same things — the pace of removing safety drivers, per-vehicle economics, and permit tightening after accidents. Regulatory adjustments in China likewise cause quarterly orders to fluctuate markedly (see Apollo Go's 2026Q2 earnings-report wording).
