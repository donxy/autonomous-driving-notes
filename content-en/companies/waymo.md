## Scale: the latest official figures

- **More than 500,000 fully driverless paid rides per week** — the latest official figure, disclosed by Alphabet CEO Pichai on 2026-04-29 in the Q1 2026 earnings; after that, the Q3 earnings on 2026-10-28 did not update the weekly ride count again. Milestone sequence: 2024-08 approx. 100,000/week (disclosed by the co-CEO on X on 2024-08-20) → 2024-10 approx. 150,000+/week (Alphabet Q3 2024 earnings call) → 2025-04 approx. 250,000/week → 2026-02 approx. 400,000/week → end of 2026-03 500,000+/week.
- "1 million per week by the end of 2026" is a **target** given by the co-CEO in 2026-02, not a figure already achieved.
- Cumulative fully driverless mileage of **more than 300 million miles** (disclosed in Alphabet's Q3 earnings on 2026-10-28); in full-year 2025 it completed approx. 15 million rides, 3 times that of 2024, with cumulative rides of more than 20 million 【Company claim】.
- Sources: [Alphabet Q1 2026 earnings](https://cdn.prod.nntech.io/company-events/reports/4427659d-5ae5-31e6-a271-a19dad46a35e/report.pdf); [Waymo official blog (2026-02-02)](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/02/waymo-raises-usd16-billion-investment-round/)

## Operating cities (15 U.S. metropolitan areas)

Alphabet's account on 2026-10-28: Phoenix, San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, San Diego, Denver, Las Vegas, Atlanta (Uber only), Austin (Uber only), Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, Orlando, Tampa, Miami, Nashville (Waymo App + Lyft). A third-party tracker counts 14 by the "open to the general public" definition (Las Vegas was invitation-only in its early opening period from 2026-09-14) — the difference in counts stems from different definitions.

Opening cadence (2025–2026): Austin 2025-03 (via Uber), Atlanta 2025-06 (via Uber); on 2025-11-12, **highway** driverless driving was opened to paying passengers; in 2026, newly added were Miami, Nashville, Dallas/Houston/San Antonio/Orlando, Denver/San Diego/Tampa (September), Las Vegas (September).

Sources: [Robotaxi tracker (checked against the Waymo Help Center on 2026-11-09)](https://thechargeport.com/robotaxi-tracker); [whereisrobotaxi](https://whereisrobotaxi.com/)

## International expansion: many announcements, few openings

- **London**: On 2025-10-15 it officially announced plans to launch a fully driverless service in 2026 (fleet operations partner Moove); as of late autumn 2026 it has **not yet opened commercially**, approx. 100 I-PACE vehicles have been testing with safety drivers since spring 2026, and it is still awaiting a UK permit.
- **Tokyo**: In 2024-12, GO/Waymo/Nihon Kotsu announced a partnership; from April 2025, Nihon Kotsu drivers have been manually collecting data; in 2026-09 the three parties agreed on a **target of within 2027** to realise Japan's first commercial L4 service; as of 2026-10 it has not opened.
- **Munich**: On 2026-08-25 it was announced as the first EU market, with a target of the end of 2027, and no German permit yet.
- How to read this: the international part is, for now, entirely at the "announced + testing" stage; view it separately from the operating scale in the U.S. mainland. Source: [Waymo official blog: London (2025-10-15)](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/10/hello-london-your-waymo-ride-is-arriving/)

- Existing mainstay: Jaguar I-PACE (5th-generation Driver).
- The Zeekr co-developed model has been renamed **Ojai** (6th-generation Driver: 13 cameras / 4 lidars / 6 radars): employee rides in February 2026, opened to part of the public in May, opened to all passengers in San Francisco/Los Angeles/Phoenix in August (approx. 300 vehicles at the time).
- Hyundai IONIQ 5 (6th-generation Driver, assembled at Hyundai's Georgia plant): partnership announced in 2024-10; as of 2026-10 it is still testing with specialists and has not yet carried passengers.
- A total fleet of approx. 4,000 vehicles (autumn 2026) is a media report based on industry roundups; "20,000 vehicles by the end of 2027" is a Semafor report citing people familiar with the matter; **Waymo declined to comment and has not confirmed it**, and this page does not cite it as fact.

## Channel partners

- **Uber**: In Austin and Atlanta, rides are available only via the Uber App, and fleet operations are handled by Avomo; according to a CNBC report, Waymo has notified that in January 2028 it will switch to its own App in the two cities (media report).
- **Lyft**: Nashville first opened via the Waymo App in 2026-04, and on 2026-09-09 the Lyft App was formally integrated — the first market where two Apps share one fleet. Source: [Lyft official blog (2026-09-09)](https://www.lyft.com/hub/posts/waymo-rides-are-on-lyft-in-nashville)
- **Moove**: Since 2024-12 it has been responsible for fleet operations (facilities, charging, operations and maintenance) in Phoenix and elsewhere, later expanding to Miami and London.

## Safety data 【Company claim: Waymo】

- Waymo Safety Impact Hub (data as of June 2026): 271.3 million driverless passenger miles; compared with the human benchmark for the same mileage, serious-injury-or-worse crashes −95%, injury crashes −82%, pedestrian injury crashes −93%. The method is based on NHTSA data and an adjusted human benchmark. Source: [Waymo Safety Impact Hub](https://waymo.com/safety/impact/)
- Cooperative research (with Waymo participating): a Swiss Re and Waymo cooperative study (Heliyon 2024) showed, within its sample, property damage claims −76% and 0 bodily injury claims observed; a follow-up study in December 2024 (25.3 million miles) found property claims −88% and bodily injury claims −92%.
- Note: all of the above are data **published or cited by the company**; the benchmark population and scenario composition are not fully comparable with its own operating domain, so retain this qualification when citing them.

## Financing and financials

- 2024-10-25: a **USD 5.6 billion** financing round, led by Alphabet. Source: [Waymo official blog](https://waymo.com/blog/2024/10/investing-to-bring-the-waymo-driver-to-more-riders/)
- 2026-02-02: a **USD 16 billion** financing round, with a post-money valuation of approx. **USD 126 billion**, with Alphabet as the main contributor.
- 2026-10-08: its first debt financing — a **USD 5 billion** syndicated term loan closed.
- Waymo does not separately disclose its results; it is counted in Alphabet's "Other Bets"; that segment's Q3 2026 revenue was USD 372 million and its operating loss was USD 2.047 billion (Alphabet earnings, 2026-10-28).
