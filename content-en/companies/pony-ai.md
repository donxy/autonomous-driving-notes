## Two Baidu people (2016–2018)

- In **December 2016**, James Peng and Tiancheng Lou founded Pony.ai in Silicon Valley. Both had previously been on Baidu's US autonomous driving team: James Peng had been Baidu's chief architect, and Tiancheng Lou had earlier worked on Google X-related projects.
- In the second half of 2017 the operations focus moved back to China, with an office set up in Nansha, Guangzhou (the headquarters was later registered in Guangzhou); the US office remained in Fremont, California.
- In February 2018, a fleet of modified Lincoln MKZ vehicles offered public test rides for the first time in Nansha, Guangzhou; PonyPilot was released in December of the same year, beginning regular robotaxi trial operations.

Source: [GlobeNewswire: Pony.ai 2018 company account](https://rss.globenewswire.com/de/news-release/2018/09/20/1573978/0/en/Pony-ai-debuts-product-ready-autonomous-vehicle-fleet.html)

## Toyota's USD 400 million and the valuation ladder (2019–2023)

- **August 2019**: a cooperation with Toyota was announced, to run pilots on public roads in China using the Lexus RX.
- **February 26, 2020**: Toyota invested **USD 400 million**; the round totalled USD 462 million, with a post-money valuation slightly above USD 3 billion — this was the starting point for Pony.ai's later mass-production cooperation with Toyota (the Gen-7 bZ4X).
- Valuation ladder: the Series C round in November 2020 rose to USD 5.3 billion; **the Series D round in March 2022 reached a peak of USD 8.5 billion**. In between, a SPAC listing planned in 2021 was shelved; in October 2023 Saudi Arabia's NEOM investment fund put in USD 100 million. Cumulative pre-IPO financing was on the order of about USD 1.2 billion (the basis varies with the scope counted).

Sources: [Crunchbase News: Toyota USD 400 million round (2020-02)](https://news.crunchbase.com/startups/pony-ai-raises-462m-with-toyota-in-the-drivers-seat/); [TechCrunch: USD 8.5 billion valuation (2022-03-07)](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/07/robotaxi-startup-pony-ai-now-valued-at-8-5b/)

## The cost in California (2021–2023)

- In May 2021, the California DMV approved it to test without a safety driver in Fremont and elsewhere (speed limited to below 45 mph); it was the eighth company to obtain this type of permit.
- On **October 28, 2021**, a driverless test vehicle in Fremont hit a central median and a road sign after turning right — a single-vehicle accident with no injuries. On **November 19, the DMV suspended its driverless testing permit** — this was the first suspension of its kind issued by the DMV (note "suspension" is not "revocation"; the two are often conflated in Chinese reporting).
- Chain reaction: in March 2022, NHTSA determined that the related software had a safety defect, and Pony.ai carried out a recall of the software used in 3 vehicles (widely reported as the first recall of autonomous driving system software); in **May 2022, the DMV, having found in its renewal review that several of its registered safety drivers had driving violation records, revoked its testing permit with a safety driver**, and for a time the company held no valid testing permit in California.
- Testing with a safety driver resumed around mid-2023; whether its driverless permit in California has been restored had, as of the date of this page, not been officially confirmed by the DMV or the company — the account stops here and makes no assertion.

Sources: [TechCrunch: permit suspension (2021-12)](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/14/pony-ai-suspension-driverless-pilot-california/); [TechCrunch: software recall (2022-03)](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/08/pony-ai-to-issue-recall-of-autonomous-driving-software/); [TechCrunch: revocation of permit with safety driver (2022-05)](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/24/pony-ai-loses-permit-to-test-autonomous-vehicles-with-driver-in-california/)

## The four-city permit marathon

- **Beijing**: in November 2021 it entered China's first commercial pilot (paid service in the Economic-Technological Development Area, with a safety driver in the main driver's seat); on April 28, 2022 it obtained, together with Baidu, one of the first "driverless" notices — this meant **no driver in the main seat but a safety driver in the front passenger seat**, not fully driverless; only in July 2023 did it obtain an "in-vehicle driverless" commercial pilot, putting fully driverless paid service in place. Writing April 2022 as the "fully driverless debut" is the most common error.
- **Guangzhou**: in April 2022 it obtained a licence to operate 100 robotaxi taxis in Nansha (the company says it was the first taxi licence for an autonomous driving company); in December 2023 it became the sole company selected for Nansha's driverless commercial mixed-traffic pilot, starting fully driverless paid service at fares consistent with Guangzhou taxis.
- **Shanghai**: in July 2023 it obtained one of Pudong's first driverless testing licences; in July 2024 it obtained a demonstration permit (free during the demonstration period); from August 1, 2025 it has operated paid service in Pudong in cooperation with Jinjiang Taxi (base fare of 14 yuan for 3 km).
- **Shenzhen**: in September 2023 it obtained the city's first driverless demonstration permit; in June 2025 it cooperated with Shenzhen Xihu, Shenzhen's largest taxi company (claiming a future deployment of more than 1,000 vehicles, a cooperation-plan basis); on October 31, 2025 it jointly obtained, with Shenzhen Xihu, Shenzhen's first **citywide** fully driverless commercial permit.
- The company claims it is "the only company holding fully driverless commercial permits in all four cities — Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou and Shenzhen" — the "company claims" qualifier is retained: the grading bases for permits differ across cities (Shanghai, for example, is demonstration operations).

Sources: [Beijing Pilot Zone release 2022-04-29](https://kfqgw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgkkfq/ztzl/cxfbqdkfq/tpxwkfq/202204/t20220429_4232051.html); [Shanghai Municipal Government: first driverless testing licences (2023-07)](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw15343/20250725/4fb45e23a1dd47b08411098851e40206.html)

## Two listings (2024–2025)

- **Listed on Nasdaq on November 27, 2024** (ticker PONY): 20 million ADSs were issued, priced at USD 13 (the top of the range), raising about USD 260 million; together with a concurrent private placement of USD 153 million, total IPO financing was about USD 413 million (BAIC subscribed for more than USD 70 million). The pricing market capitalisation was about USD 4.55 billion — nearly half the USD 8.5 billion primary-market peak valuation of 2022. Get the order right: **WeRide had already listed on October 25, 2024**, so Pony.ai was the second that year, and the claim of "the world's first listed robotaxi company" does not hold.
- **Listed on the HKEX Main Board on November 6, 2025** (code 2026), completing a dual primary listing in US and Hong Kong stocks: the issue price was 139 HKD, with base proceeds of about 6.71 billion HKD (about USD 860 million); if the over-allotment option is exercised in full, the ceiling is about 7.7 billion HKD — the two figures are used interchangeably in reporting, so state the basis when citing.

Sources: [Nasdaq: Pony.ai listing announcement (2024-11-27)](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/ponyai-lists-nasdaq-2024-11-27); [TechCrunch: USD 4.55 billion pricing market capitalisation](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/27/pony-ai-set-for-nasdaq-debut-at-4-55b-valuation/)

## Gen-7 and the thousand-vehicle pledge (2025)

- On **April 23, 2025** the seventh-generation (Gen-7) system and three mass-production models were unveiled at the Shanghai Auto Show: the Toyota bZ4X, the BAIC ARCFOX Alpha T5, and the second-generation GAC AION V; the configuration includes 9 lidars, 14 cameras and 4 mmWave radars.
- Cost claims on a company basis: the kit is 100% automotive-grade, BOM cost is down 70% versus the previous generation, and the design life is 10 years / 600,000 km — only a relative reduction is given, with no absolute price (see the basis note in [[market/business-model|Business Model]]).
- Mass-production progress (financial-report disclosure): the GAC model rolled off the line in June 2025 and the ARCFOX T5 model in July; as of November 23, 2025 the total fleet was 961 vehicles (of which 667 were Gen-7), delivering most of its "1,000 vehicles by the end of 2025" target.
- The company says Gen-7 has achieved city-level per-vehicle UE break-even in Guangzhou (average daily net revenue per vehicle of 299 yuan, a management calculation in the financial report, not an audited conclusion).

Source: [Nasdaq: Gen-7 unveiling (2025-04)](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/pony-ai-inc-unveils-seventh-generation-autonomous-driving-system-and-robotaxi-lineup)

## A year of overseas signings (2025)

2025 was Pony.ai's concentrated signing year, with high density, but signings and actual deployment must be viewed separately:

- **Dubai**: an MoU was signed with the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) on May 26, 2025 (the vehicle unveiling ceremony in early July is often miswritten as the signing date); a road-testing permit was obtained on September 26 and pilot testing began; the company plans to launch fully driverless commercial service in 2026.
- **Qatar**: in cooperation with the national transport company Mowasalat on September 5, 2025, road testing began in Doha (initially with a safety driver), followed by a paid pilot with a safety driver via the Karwa App.
- **Platforms and automakers**: in May 2025 it announced that its robotaxi would join the Uber platform (the Middle East first); on October 17 it signed a **non-binding** MoU with Stellantis to integrate the system into its medium van platform, testing in Luxembourg first.
- **Luxembourg**: the European headquarters is located here; an L4 road-testing permit was obtained in April 2025.

- A structural reminder: robotaxi is not yet its largest revenue source — in 2025 Q2, truck (Robotruck) service revenue was USD 9.5 million and licensing and applications revenue was USD 10.4 million, both above robotaxi's USD 1.5 million; robotaxi rose to USD 6.7 million only in Q3. Post-listing financial reports no longer separately disclose cumulative order volume, disclosing only fare revenue and registered-user growth.
- Latest in 2026 (financial-report disclosure): total revenue in Q1 was USD 34.3 million (up +145% year on year), robotaxi revenue was USD 8.6 million; on 2026-05-26 the company raised its 2026 fleet target from 3,000+ to more than 3,500 vehicles (Q1 2026 financial report). In Q3 (released 2026-11-18): total revenue was USD 44.6 million (up +75.2% year on year), robotaxi revenue was USD 17.8 million (up +165.4% year on year) and accounted for about 40% of total revenue, of which paid fare revenue grew by nearly 400% year on year; as of the end of September 2026 the fleet was 2,768 vehicles, the year-end target was reiterated at 3,500 vehicles, deployed in 20 cities worldwide; the Q3 GAAP net loss was USD 136.3 million, mainly due to fair-value losses on trading securities, while the non-GAAP net loss was USD 48.9 million, narrowing year on year. The financial report still did not disclose order volume.

Sources: [Pony.ai 2025 Q3 financial report](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/50350/2025/RNS/50350_rns_2025-11-25_a771f3a8-4ff6-416b-966a-a275296ee519.pdf); [Pony.ai 2026 Q3 financial report (GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-18)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/18/3405413/0/en/pony-ai-inc-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-total-revenues-up-75-2-yoy-to-us-44-6-million-with-robotaxi-services-revenues-growing-by-165-4-yoy.html)
